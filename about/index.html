About this app

How the inspection forms are built and how they reach a tablet or phone.

In plain terms

The inspection forms are ordinary web pages. The Site Admin controls and edits them with assistance from company-approved AI agents, and tests and approves every change before it goes live. GitHub, a free service, stores every version and publishes the latest one at a web address. Each technician opens that address once on a tablet or phone; the forms then save to the device and keep working with no signal in the yard. Inspections and photos stay on the device and leave only as the PDF report or email the technician sends. Approved updates reach a device the next time it opens the app online, and every form shows its revision number and date at the bottom so anyone can check which version they have.

The path of one change

  1. 1 · Site Admin and AI agents

    Ask and build

    The Site Admin describes what the floor needs. An approved AI agent writes or edits the form and tests it before showing it.

  2. 2 · Site Admin

    Test and approve

    The change is tried on a real tablet. Nothing goes live until the Site Admin approves it.

  3. 3 · GitHub

    Store and publish

    GitHub keeps every version, so a bad change can be rolled back. It also hosts the live web address.

  4. 4 · Tablets and phones

    Use in the shop

    Technicians open the address, and the forms run on the device, online or off. Reports leave as PDF or email.

Who does what

Site AdminDecides what the app does, tests it, and approves every release.
AI agentsWrite and test the code at the Site Admin's direction. They publish nothing without the Site Admin's approval.
GitHubStores the files and their history and serves them to the devices.

What it is not

There is no company server or database behind the app. Inspection records are not uploaded anywhere by the app. They sit on the technician's device until a report is sent.
