Quick Start

One idea, then how to find your way. Enter the truck's data once and let the QR code carry it forward.

The one idea

Every form makes a QR code (it is on the report PDF and on the folder front page). The next form scans it, so the serial number, VIN, plate and other core data are already filled in. Scan, check it against the truck, carry on. Do not retype.

Where a truck goes

The workflow page shows each path in full. In short:

Repair
Intake›Post-Repair›Report emailed
Upfit / upgrade
Intake›Floor Test›Jake's Final›Calibration (if a camera, TFU or radar changed)›ATP›Punch list and audit
New build
New build intake›Floor Test›Jake's Final›ATP

New build: the Floor Test link to the truck's QR is still being built. Until then, type the serial in the Floor Test.

How to scan

  1. On the home screen tap Scan a QR code, or choose 📷 Scan a QR code instead when starting a new inspection.
  2. Point the tablet at the QR on the previous report or the folder front page.
  3. Check what filled in against the truck. If a value is wrong, fix it. The form uses what you confirm.

The forms

Intake (short)Core data, customer-reported issues and a main-functions check. Also starts a new build.
Intake (long)The longer intake.
Post-RepairCloses out each repair on the work list.
Floor TestBench test of the skid devices. Device serials are captured here.
Jake's FinalFinal check before the ATP.
CalibrationTFU and radar calibration.
ATPThe acceptance test. Failed lines become the punch list.
SkyWatchSkyWatch inspection and completion certificate.

Colors

Orange is a reminder or warning. It never blocks you. Red is only for a real fault.

If something goes wrong

  • Forms save as you go. If the page freezes or you lose your place, reload it and reopen the inspection from Home.
  • No signal? The forms work without a connection once they are saved to the tablet. Get a connection once and keep the page open a few seconds. Tap Get all forms if you will be offline.
  • Do not clear the browser's data. Unsent inspections live on the tablet until you send the report. Send reports soon.
  • Dictation not working? Use the keyboard's own microphone key instead. Dictation works best in Chrome on the Samsung tablets and can be less steady on iPhone.
  • Stuck? Take a photo of the screen and send it to Ravi.

Small things that save time

  • Name fields start blank and offer the names recently used on that tablet.
  • Any field you filled in offers your earlier entries again on the same tablet.
  • Take photos with the tablet's camera. The form keeps them with the right item.
  • Failed repair reports go to TSC@teledyne.com. Passed reports go to the customer.
