Not part of any form. Nothing here is saved. This is the new dictation for the forms: one beep when you tap a field. When you pause and the tablet stops listening, it asks “Is that everything?” – tap Yes, done, Keep going (adds more, one beep) or Start over. You can also tap ✓ Done any time while it listens.

To fix something in the middle: tap the spot in the field (or use ◀ ▶), ⌫ deletes the character before the cursor, then 🎤 Speak here – it goes in at the cursor. Clear (on the right) empties the field.

Try reading a whole VIN with pauses, e.g. “one foxtrot tango seven … whiskey two bravo alpha … two tango echo charlie”.
