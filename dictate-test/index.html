Tips for speaking codes

Say one character at a time with a short gap. For letters, the call-sign words work best: alpha, bravo, charlie … x-ray. “Zero” or “oh” becomes 0; say oscar for the letter O. “Dash” adds -, “point” or “dot” adds ., “double five” gives 55.

Microphone check

Shows whether the tablet hears you at all. If this bar moves but dictation gives nothing back, the microphone is fine and it is the browser’s speech service that is missing.

Clean-up check (no speaking needed)

Type what the dictation might hear. The result is what would go into the field.
